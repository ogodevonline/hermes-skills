# Pobeda (DP) check-in & boarding pass rules

Sources: https://www.flypobeda.ru/information/registration , https://www.flypobeda.ru/services/checkin , https://www.flypobeda.ru/information/transport-regulations (гл. 7). Re-check if a decision hinges on it.

## Online check-in
- Only for passengers with a selected seat («Выгодный»/«Максимум» or paid «Выбор места»). «Базовый» without seat → desk check-in only.
- Opens 24 h before; closes 2 h before at most airports (3 h Tashkent, Sheremetyevo etc.; 4 h Vnukovo).
- After online check-in the boarding pass must be PRINTED, except departures where mobile is accepted: Vnukovo (all flights), ~25 Russian cities (domestic only), Alanya, Minsk, Istanbul. Uzbekistan airports are not on the list; Tashkent/Osh/Antalya/Gyumri print at the desk only.

## Airport desk
- Needs only the passport the ticket was issued on — itinerary printout not required.
- Opens 2 h before at most airports (3 h: Tashkent, Samarkand, Sheremetyevo, Osh, Istanbul…; 4 h Vnukovo); closes 40 min before (60 min Istanbul/Dubai/Abu Dhabi).
- Boarding closes 25 min before (20 min Vnukovo/Sheremetyevo/Sochi).
- Agencies warn desk check-in abroad "may cost up to €60"; a 2019 Moscow court ruled Pobeda's paid desk check-in abroad unlawful — current status per airport unverified, say so.

## Baggage on «Базовый»
- One cabin item 36×30×27 cm, weight unlimited; checked bag not included.
